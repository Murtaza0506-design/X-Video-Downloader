# Outreach — structure

For messaging a hiring manager or a contact directly, which converts far
better than the portal and is the main reason to keep the tracker's
`contact_name` column.

- **Under 120 words.** Anything longer is read as a pitch and skimmed.
- **Say how you got to them** — the posting, a mutual contact, something
  they published.
- **One line of relevance**, with a number in it.
- **One specific ask** — fifteen minutes, or a pointer to the right person.
  Not "any advice you might have".
- **No attachment on the first message.**

Follow up once, after about a week. Then stop; a second follow-up costs
more than it returns.
