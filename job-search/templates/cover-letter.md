# Cover letter — structure, not boilerplate

A generic letter is worth less than no letter. This is a shape to fill,
and if the blanks can't be filled specifically, the fit check was too
generous.

**Paragraph 1 — the specific opening.** What this organisation is doing
that this role sits inside, and why that is the one you're writing to.
Named, concrete, and impossible to paste into another application. No "I
am writing to apply for".

**Paragraph 2 — the strongest evidence.** The single closest thing you
have actually done, with its numbers. One example told properly beats
three listed.

**Paragraph 3 — the bridge.** If the CV doesn't obviously match, this is
where it gets addressed directly — the sector switch, the missing title,
the gap. Stated plainly and then moved past. A screener who has to guess
at it assumes the worse answer.

**Paragraph 4 — close.** What you want to happen next. Short.

Constraints: under a page; no adjectives doing work that evidence should
do; no claim that isn't on the CV.
