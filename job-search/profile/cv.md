# Master CV

> **Empty — this is the blocker.** Nothing in this workspace produces useful
> output until the real CV lives here. Paste it in raw form (copy out of the
> PDF/Word file, formatting damage is fine) or drop the file into the repo
> and it will be transcribed into this file.
>
> Raw and complete beats tidy and partial. Specifically worth keeping:
>
> - Every role: employer, job title, start and end dates (month/year), and
>   whether it was permanent, contract, or interim.
> - What each role actually involved, in whatever words you already have.
> - Numbers wherever they exist — budget, headcount, users, duration,
>   volumes, savings, dates hit. These are the currency of a PM CV and they
>   are the thing most CVs are missing.
> - Education, certifications (PRINCE2, PMP, APM, Scrum, SAFe, Prosci...),
>   and any part-completed ones.
> - Tools you have genuinely used.
> - Anything unusual: career breaks, sector switches, the layoff, side
>   projects. These are handled far better when planned for than when
>   discovered by an interviewer.
